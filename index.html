Mr. Mayor, here is how we convince people.

You asked me how to convince people that a smart city is good for their way of life. My answer: don’t talk to them about sensors. Show them their own day.

This is our town today: grey, polluted, stuck in traffic. Press the switch and watch it wake up, street by street. Then follow one resident from 7:30 in the morning to 10:30 at night.
