From the IT department · to the mayor's office

Good evening, Mr. Mayor,

You asked your IT technician whether our town should become a smart city. Instead of slides, I built you a model.

Right now it is an ordinary town. Lights turn red for empty streets. Bins overflow on one corner while the truck empties half-empty ones on the next. Streetlights burn all night for nobody.

Walk around it with me. You asked three questions:

  • IWhich problems can a smart city solve?
  • IIHow can we be sure the data is safe?
  • IIIHow do we convince people?
Yanis
IT technician, town hall
